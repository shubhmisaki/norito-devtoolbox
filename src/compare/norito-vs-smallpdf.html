norito vs Smallpdf

Smallpdf is polished and capable. norito skips the upload step and the usage caps. Here's how they compare.

Smallpdf is a polished, well-designed suite of PDF and file tools with a strong free tier for occasional single tasks. It's a solid choice if you want a slick interface and don't mind uploading your document to use it.

norito covers the same everyday PDF operations (compress, merge, split, convert, watermark, sign, extract text) plus a much larger library of image, developer, text, SEO, and design tools, all running client-side. Nothing you process on norito ever reaches a server.

When Smallpdf makes more sense

  • You want e-signature requests sent to other people, not just signing yourself.
  • You need PDF editing with a full WYSIWYG text/image editor rather than the more targeted operations norito offers.
  • You're already paying for their Pro plan and it's integrated into your workflow.

When norito makes more sense

  • You want to keep a document private and skip the upload step entirely.
  • You need more than PDF tools, image compression, QR codes, JSON formatting, and 45+ other utilities are all in the same place.
  • You don't want to hit a free-tier wall on your second or third file of the day.

Common questions

Does norito have as many features as Smallpdf?

For core PDF operations (merge, split, compress, convert, watermark, sign, extract text, add page numbers), yes. Smallpdf has some editing and e-signature-request features norito doesn't. In exchange, norito never uploads your file and has no usage limits.

Why is norito free with no limits?

Because there's no server-side processing to pay for, your browser does the work. The site covers its costs with minimal advertising instead of a subscription.

Is norito safe for sensitive documents?

Since files never leave your device, there's no upload, no server storage, and nothing to leak from norito's side. That said, always use your own judgment with sensitive material regardless of the tool.
