Common questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript, the files and text you work with are processed on your device and never uploaded anywhere. You can check this yourself: open your browser's Network tab while using any tool and you won't see your file leave your machine.

Why is norito free?

Ads (via Google AdSense) cover hosting costs. There's no premium tier, and no plan to put any tool behind a paywall.

Do you store or see my files?

No. There's no server component behind the tools, so there's nothing on our end that could store, log, or even see what you upload.

What happens when I close the tab?

Everything's gone. Nothing is saved except a few interface preferences, like your last-used output format, stored in your browser's local storage, which also never leaves your device.

Do you use cookies?

norito's own code doesn't set any. Google AdSense may set advertising cookies, details are in the Privacy Policy.
